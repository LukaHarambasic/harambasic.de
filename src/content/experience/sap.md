    content: |
      To be honest, I used to think "SAP" was just the name of the arena where the Rhein-Neckar Löwen played handball. I had zero idea what the company actually did until my grandma took me to their Annual General Meeting. That led to a voluntary internship in 12th grade, which turned into something I would have never imagined: leading parts of a project for all of Germany.

      Fast forward to 2020: I went from student to Android Lead for Germany's official COVID tracing app (Corona-Warn-App) in about 50 days. After the initial release I got to lead the team, managing 10+ developers under a lot of pressure.

      - 48 Million+ Downloads: Launched and maintained the app half the country used to trace infection chains.
      - 0 to 1 in 50 Days: Built critical public health software with no initial requirements, shipping production hotfixes for millions of users in under 6 hours.
      - Open Source Wins: I was terrified our code would be torn apart publicly, but we built an amazing community. One of the most active and most knowledgeable members turned out to be a 16-year-old student. As a little thank you, I helped organize an internship for him.

      The whole thing is still public: [coronawarn.app](https://www.coronawarn.app/en/), [GitHub](https://github.com/corona-warn-app/cwa-app-android), [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.rki.coronawarnapp), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/corona-warn-app/id1512595757).

      It was a weird time during Covid. We stayed on calls just to hear each other type, played "Among Us" for team building (I always got killed first), and my roommate literally cooked and brought food to my desk so I could keep working. Danke Amdi :)
