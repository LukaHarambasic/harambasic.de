A lot of people I talk to feel like they're behind on AI. Looking at the numbers Notion just published, they probably aren't.

Notion surveyed 6118 individuals: 88% believe their organisations are at Level 1 or 2.

The levels as Notion defines them:

- Level 1: AI as a thought partner (standalone tools)
- Level 2: AI as an assistant (embedded in daily work)
- Level 3: AI as teammates (agents automating workflows)
- Level 4: AI as the system (agents running business processes autonomously)

To be fair, with Notion's heavy AI focus they have every reason to tell you it's early and you're not behind. So take this quote with a grain of salt:

> "The worry about being behind is nearly universal, and for most organisations it's misplaced. Early is the baseline, not the exception." [1]

That matches what I see though. Most people I know open ChatGPT or Claude, ask something, get an answer. That's Level 1. And that's fine. Not everyone wants to sink hours into playing with the newest thing, figure out it's way more complex than expected, and realise they could have done it faster manually.

Everyone is still at the beginning of what people believe is possible here. You're not alone, and you're not late.

PS: I'm also a sucker for a clean 4-level model like this one.
