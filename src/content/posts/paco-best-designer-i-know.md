I've worked with a lot of designers. Some are brilliant at aesthetics but get lost in strategy. Others understand the business but miss the craft. Paco does all of it.

He's been at Moonboon as a Lead Product Designer, and I've watched him work through projects that require real discipline: shipping fast, solving for real users, and doing it without letting the quality slip. That's harder than it sounds.

What sets him apart is the clarity. When Paco presents a design decision, it's anchored. Not "this looks good" but "users need this path because of X, so we optimized for Y." He thinks in systems, not one-off pages. He can move between high-level strategy and pixel-level detail without losing the thread.

He also actually ships. There's a lot of beautiful design work that never reaches users. Paco designs the thing that gets built and used. He knows the constraints, the timeline, the engineering reality, and works inside that while still pushing the bar higher.

I also know he's built Lovable websites for actual products (his parents' construction company got one). That's the move of someone who understands the full cycle: not just making it beautiful, but making it work end-to-end.

Copenhagen's got good designers. Paco's the one I'd want in the room when it matters.
